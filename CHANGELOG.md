# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public release.

- Guided capture with a split-screen and a conversation layout, 15 questions
- Register with multi-select filters for process area, process and person, plus charts that filter the page
- Priority question and priority chart, with choices read from the sheet's dropdown
- Roles, access requests, approval queue and an Add a person form
- Audit log for changes made in the app and in the sheet
- Process area guide inside the capture questionnaire
- Live sync, edit conflict protection and plain-language error messages
- Clickable prototype with sample data and a What to try panel
